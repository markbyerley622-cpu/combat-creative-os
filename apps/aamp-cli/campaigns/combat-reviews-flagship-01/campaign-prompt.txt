Combat Reviews flagship advertisement — 15 seconds, vertical, internal review.

Combat sports fans follow more than one promotion. The cards are on different
nights, on different broadcasters, in different time zones, and the schedule
lives in whichever group chat somebody remembered to post in. So people find
out a fight happened after it happened.

Combat Reviews puts the events, the fight cards, the predictions, the
leaderboard and the card-talk discussion in one place.

Make one cut that does three things in order: land the tension, show the real
product doing the thing, and end on a call to action that is true.

Territory: watching is only the beginning.

Constraints that are not negotiable:

- Show the real Combat Reviews interface. The product is the proof, and a
  legible screen beats a decorative one.
- Say nothing the product cannot back. No event counts, no fighter names in
  authored copy, no vote totals, no countdowns, no store badges, no download
  promise. Where a real capture shows real data, let the capture speak.
- Black, deep neutral, Combat Reviews red and white. One display type system.
  Controlled glow, high-contrast opening frame, a clean CTA hold.
- The prediction feature is skill, not betting, and the cut must never read as
  a betting product.
- End on: NEVER MISS FIGHT NIGHT. / OPEN COMBAT REVIEWS.

Every transition earns its place by narrative or visual motivation. No generic
template wipes.
