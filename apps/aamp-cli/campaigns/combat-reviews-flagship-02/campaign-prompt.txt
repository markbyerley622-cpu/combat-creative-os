Combat Reviews flagship advertisement, locked ten-panel storyboard.

The storyboard is the art direction. It is not a suggestion and it is not a
starting point: its scene order, compositions, headlines, palette, phone
placement, combat-to-product relationship, call to action and energy are all
locked. This cut animates it and improves it. It does not reinterpret it.

Ten scenes, fifteen seconds, no gaps: a push notification lands, the breadth of
the sport opens up, the events interface arrives, the rankings are researched,
two fighters are compared, a free prediction is made, the prediction is
submitted on a bell, the predictor's record improves, the fans argue, and the
brand resolves on one instruction.

Constraints that are not negotiable:

- Do not reorder, combine, remove or reinterpret a scene.
- Do not replace a storyboard scene with unrelated footage because that footage
  happens to be available.
- Say nothing the product cannot back. Where the storyboard asserts something
  unverifiable, change only that element and leave the scene alone.
- Every phone screen in the storyboard is concept UI. Declare it as such.
- No store badges, no download promise, no invented performance claim.
- End on: NEVER MISS FIGHT NIGHT. / EXPLORE EVENTS.

This is an internal-review motion proof. It is not licensed public-production
media and it is not public-release ready.
